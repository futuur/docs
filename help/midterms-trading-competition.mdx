Free to play: no purchase or deposit needed. The top 5 by profit split 550 USDC.

- **Period:** October 14, 2026, 00:00 UTC to November 30, 2026, 23:59 UTC
- **Eligible markets:** everything tagged Midterms at [https://futuur.com/tags/midterm](https://futuur.com/tags/midterm)
- **Entry:** automatic. Trade eligible markets during the period and you're in.
- **Currency:** only trades in Ooms count - Ooms are play money with no cash value. AI agents and API trading are welcome.

## Who qualifies

You must meet all three to be ranked:

1. At least 10 distinct eligible markets traded, each resolved within the period.
2. One account per person, active and in good standing when the competition ends.
3. You're 18 or older and not located in an excluded jurisdiction (see below).

## How the ranking works

- Ranked by net profit in Ooms: the absolute amount, not % return.
- Profit = returns − amount staked, counting only positions opened during the period in markets that resolve during the period.
- Open positions at the end don't count: neither their returns nor their stake.
- Voided or cancelled markets don't count, for profit or for the 10-market minimum.
- Tiebreak: higher ROI (profit ÷ total staked). If still tied, whoever reached their final profit first.
- The official ranking is on this page, updated daily.

## One account per person

- You compete with one account, no matter how many you hold.
- If we find multiple accounts from the same person, all of them are disqualified, including the main one.
- Moving value between your own accounts is prohibited.
- We use platform signals to detect linked accounts. Futuur makes the final call.

## Who can't win

Futuur employees, contractors and their immediate family (spouse, parents, children, siblings) can trade these markets but aren't ranked and can't win prizes. The same applies to candidates in any race covered by an eligible market, their paid campaign staff, and officials who administer or count votes in those races.

## Payout and general rules

- Winners are announced within 3 business days after the end date.
- Winners are paid in USDC or USDT within 7 days of the announcement
- We may ask winners to verify their identity, age, location and eligibility, and to provide tax information, before paying.
- Participation may be restricted in some jurisdictions.
- Using a VPN to hide your location means disqualification.
- Futuur may disqualify anyone for market manipulation, collusion, multiple accounts or attempts to game these rules.
- We may adjust or end the competition after a technical failure or an event outside our control, with notice on the competition page.
- Futuur's decisions are final.
- Payout will be done in either of the following networks and currencies USDC or USDT:
  1. USDT.ERC20
  2. USDT.BEP20
  3. USDC.ERC20
  4. USDC.BASE

You're responsible for providing a correct wallet address on the network you choose. Funds sent to a wrong address or network can't be recovered.

## Is this connected to any election authority or campaign?

No. The competition isn't sponsored, endorsed or administered by any government, election authority, candidate, campaign or party, and nothing in it rewards voting or not voting.

## Will my username be published?

The leaderboard shows usernames and scores. Winners agree that Futuur may publish their username, and final rank in announcements and marketing, except where the law prohibits it.

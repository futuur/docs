Free to play: no purchase or deposit needed. The top 5 by profit split 550 USDC.

- **Period:** October 14, 2026, 00:00 UTC to November 30, 2026, 23:59 UTC
- **Eligible markets:** everything tagged Midterms at [https://futuur.com/tags/midterm](https://futuur.com/tags/midterm)
- **Entry:** automatic. Trade eligible markets during the period and you're in.
- **Currency:** only trades in Ooms count - Ooms are play money with no cash value. AI agents and API trading are welcome.

## Who qualifies

You must meet all three to be ranked:

1. At least 10 distinct eligible markets traded, each resolved within the period.
2. One account per person, active and in good standing when the competition ends.
3. You're 18 or older and not located in an excluded jurisdiction (see below).

## How the ranking works

- Ranked by net profit in Ooms: the absolute amount, not % return.
- Profit = returns − amount staked, counting only positions opened during the period in markets that resolve during the period.
- Open positions at the end don't count: neither their returns nor their stake.
- Voided or cancelled markets don't count, for profit or for the 10-market minimum.
- Tiebreak: higher ROI (profit ÷ total staked). If still tied, whoever reached their final profit first.
- The official ranking is on this page, updated daily.

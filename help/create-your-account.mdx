Here you find guidance on creating an account on Futuur and signing in.

## How do I create an account on Futuur?

Start by heading off to [https://futuur.com/l/register](https://futuur.com/l/register). Creating an account and signing in happen on the same screen: the first time you sign in, your account is created.

**1. Choose how to sign in**

You can sign in in three ways:

- **Continue with Google**: use your Google account.
- **Continue with Wallet**: connect a crypto wallet and sign a message to prove it is yours.
- **Email**: type your email address and click **Continue**. We send a one-time code to that address; enter the code to sign in.

There is no password to create or remember. Each time you sign in with email, you receive a new code.

<Tip>
  Always sign in the same way. Your account is tied to the email address or wallet you first used, so signing in with a different email address or a different wallet creates a separate, new account.
</Tip>

**2. Choose your username**

New accounts start with an automatically generated username, and you are asked to pick your own. Your username is public on Futuur and is part of the address of your profile.

If you pick a username such as “tom”, for example, your profile on Futuur will be at [https://futuur.com/tom](https://futuur.com/tom), so it is easy to share with your friends.

You can change your username later from your profile. Your profile address changes with it. Links with your old username keep working until someone else takes that username.

**3. Set your profile picture**

Your profile looks much better with a picture, right? Pick your favorite shot and let it shine!

Your profile picture doesn’t have to show your face, and this is not a mandatory step.

Now you are ready to browse markets on Futuur and place bets in play money (Ooms). Every new account starts with 10,000 Ooms.

## Do I need to verify my email address?

You need a confirmed email address to make a real-money deposit.

- If you signed in with **email**, the one-time code you entered already confirmed your address. There is nothing else to do.
- If you signed in with a **wallet**, your account has no email address yet. You will be asked to add one before your first deposit. Futuur uses it for notifications and to confirm your withdrawals.

If a message from Futuur does not arrive, please check your Spam folder or get in touch with [support@futuur.com](mailto:support@futuur.com).

## Can I change my email address after I finish the registration?

Yes. Head to your [Account Settings page](https://futuur.com/a/settings) and enter the new address under **Notification email**. We send a confirmation link to the new address.

Your current address keeps working until you confirm the new one. After that, the new address becomes your sign-in email, and any connected social logins (such as Google) are disconnected.

If you no longer have access to your old email address and cannot sign in, please contact [support@futuur.com](mailto:support@futuur.com).

Here you find guidance on the process of creating an account on Futuur<br />
using any of our login options.

## How do I create an account on Futuur?

Start by heading off to [https://futuur.com/l/register](https://futuur.com/l/register) and choosing your registration method

**1. Select your registration method**

You can create your account by either:

- Associating your Google account to Futuur
- Entering your email and choosing a password
- Associating you Facebook account to Futuur

If you choose to sign up with email, your password must have at least 8 characters, and include both letters and numbers.

**2. Choose your username**

Take your time and choose wisely, as your username will be public on Futuur, and will be reflected on the url of your profile.

If you pick a username such as “tom”, for example, your profile on Futuur will be hosted at [https://futuur.com/tom](https://futuur.com/tom) , so it becomes easy to share it with your friends.

You cannot change your username once you select it. If you make a mistake in that process, please contact [support@futuur.com](mailto:support@futuur.com) so our team can assist you.

**3. Set your profile picture**

Your profile looks much better with a picture, right? Pick your favorite shot and let it shine!

Your profile picture doesn’t have to show your face, and this is not a mandatory step.

If you prefer, you can skip this step and head directly to the markets.

Now, you are ready to browse markets on Futuur and place bets in play money (Ooms). You can still check your email to improve your account.

**Verify your account**

Regardless of which registration method you selected, you must verify your email address to use all of Futuur’s features.

You will not be allowed to use real money on Futuur (be it to make deposits, place bets, sell positions or withdraw funds) until you verify your email address.

If you decide to skip this step for the moment and head directly to the markets feed, you will only be allowed to interact with markets with play money (Ooms).

To verify your email address, please head to your email inbox and click on the link inside the message you received from Futuur.

If you do not find the message in your inbox, please check your Spam folder or get in touch with [support@futuur.com](mailto:support@futuur.com)

## How can I verify my email address later on?

If you decided to skip the verification process at the moment you created your account, you have 24 hours to complete it by clicking on the link sent to your inbox. Once that deadline expires, you can request a the verification message to be sent again to your inbox.

To summarize the verification process, simply try to make a deposit or place a bet in real money, and you will be prompted instructions for verify.

## Can I change my email address after I finish the registration?

If you no longer have access to the email address you informed when you created your account — or in case your mistyped it during account creation —, please head to your [Account Settings page](https://futuur.com/a/settings), click on the option to [Update Email Address](https://futuur.com/p/email-update), and resume the verification process with you updated address.

Everything you need to quote on Futuur through the API. Start in play money, go live when you're ready.

## 1. Get started

1. **Create an account** and generate your API keys under **Settings → API keys**: [futuur.com/a/settings](https://futuur.com/a/settings)
2. **Test in play money.** Add `currency_mode=play_money` to your queries and use the `OOM` currency when placing orders. It runs on the same endpoints as real money, at no cost. Ooms can't be withdrawn, so play money proves your integration, not your P&L.
3. **Fund a real-money account** if you're in an eligible jurisdiction (see section 6).
4. **Go live** with `currency_mode=real_money` and `USDC`.

## 2. API at a glance

Full documentation: [docs.futuur.com](/introduction) · Base URL: `https://api.futuur.com/v2.0`

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | HMAC-SHA512. Send `Key`, `Timestamp` and `HMAC` headers. [Authentication guide](/authentication) |
| Browse events and order books | `GET /events/`, and the order book endpoint per market |
| Place an order | `POST /orders/`. Limit order with `price` between 0 and 1, or market order with `price: null` |
| Order size | Minimum bet in real money is 1 USD; no minimum in Ooms. Maximum varies by market. |
| Batch operations | Create, update or cancel **up to 20 orders per request** |
| Cancel | Cancel one order, a batch, or **all orders** (optionally scoped to one event or market) |
| Your rebates | `GET /wagers/rebates/` |

Useful order options: `expired_at` auto-cancels the unfilled part of an order at a set time; `cancel_conflicting_orders` cancels your conflicting open orders on the same market before placing the new one.

**Real-time data.** Futuur uses Pusher WebSockets: the global `event` channel for market-wide feeds, `event-{event_id}` for best-bid/offer and order-book updates, and HMAC-authorized private channels for your orders and account. [WebSocket docs](/api-reference/websocket/overview)

## 3. Rate limits

- **2,000 requests per minute** and **30,000 per day**, per account.
- **Higher limits are available to market makers on request.** Write to [makers@futuur.com.](mailto:makers@futuur.com)
- Use WebSockets for market data rather than polling, and batch endpoints for order changes.
- Identical POST requests within 1 second are rejected as duplicates, so wait at least 1 second before retrying.

[Rate limits docs](/rate-limits)

## 4. Fees and rebates

The taker pays a fee; **80% of it goes back to the maker** whose resting order was filled, instantly and on every market. No application or volume threshold. There’s zero fees for makers.

[Rebates](/help/rebates): how it works, with an example

## 5. Deposits, withdrawals and settlement

- **Assets:** USDC for real money. Ooms for play money (not withdrawable).
- **Deposits:** on-chain transfer. Make sure the network you select on Futuur matches the network you send from. Typically 10–30 minutes. Futuur charges no deposit fee.
- **Withdrawals:** Wallet → Withdraw. You confirm each request by email, then it is reviewed manually, generally within 24 hours. Funds usually arrive within 30 minutes of approval.

| Asset and network | Withdrawal fee | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDC · Polygon | None | 1 USDC |
| USDC · Solana | None | 1 USDC |
| USDC · Ethereum (ERC-20) | 5 USDC | 10 USDC |

- **KYC:** proof of identity and address may be required once cumulative deposits or withdrawals exceed the equivalent of \$2,000.
- **Market settlement:** a market closes to new bets and sales at its Bet End Date, and pays out when it is resolved under its published rules. Sports markets are resolved automatically through a data provider, generally within 12 hours after the match. Other markets are resolved manually by our editorial team, and timing varies.

More: [Deposits and Withdrawals](/help/deposits-and-withdrawals) · [Rules and resolutions](/help/rules-and-resolutions)

## 6. Where you can trade real money

Play money is available everywhere. Real-money trading is **not available** in certain jurisdictions, including the **United States, United Kingdom, Canada, France, Germany, Australia, Japan and Singapore**. See the full list and our licence details on [License and Jurisdiction](/help/license-and-jurisdiction).

Trading as a firm? Write to [support@futuur.com](mailto:support@futuur.com) and we'll walk you through onboarding.

## 7. Order book and AMM

Futuur runs one order book per market. Futuur's automated market maker (AMM) is a participant in that book: it posts its own buy and sell orders there, like any other maker.

- **Market orders** match the orders resting in the book, best price first. At the same price, another user's order fills before the AMM's. Whatever cannot be matched is cancelled: a market order never rests in the book.
- **Limit orders** stay in the book until they are filled, you cancel them, or they expire.
- **If the book is empty,** a market order has nothing to match and is cancelled. Use a limit order instead.

More: [Order Book](/help/order-book) · [Automated Market Maker](/help/automated-market-maker)

## 8. What's coming

- **Liquidity rewards program.** Rewards for resting liquidity, in design. We're putting together a small first group of market makers to try the platform and help shape it. Interested? Write to [makers@futuur.com](mailto:makers@futuur.com).

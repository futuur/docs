## The Information We Collect

### Information You Provide to Us

1. We may collect personal information from you, such as your name, physical and email address, username and password, and the country, state and address in which you reside, as well as photo and username that you provide for your public profile page ("Profile") when you create an App account.
2. When you make a forecast or otherwise conduct activity on the App, or deposit or withdraw funds from your Account, we may collect all information necessary to complete the transaction, including your name, address, phone number, credit or debit card information, billing information and social security number.
3. When make a forecast on the App the other forecasters may be able to see the information in your Profile.
4. If you provide us feedback, suggestions, make inquiries or contact us via email, we will collect your name and email address, as well as any other content included in the email, in order to send you a reply.
5. If you tell us where you are (e.g., by allowing your mobile device to send us your location), we may store and use that information to verify for eligibility to make forecasts and for fraud prevention purposes.
6. When you participate in one of our surveys, we may collect additional profile information.
7. If you make or propose a bet on the App, we may ask you for your email address and/or home number (to notify you whether you win or not). We may also ask for first and last names, and sometimes post office addresses to verify your identity. In some situations we may need additional information as part of the entry process or any contest we run.
8. We may also collect information related to your views and predictions on a number of subjects, and other information you may voluntarily submit to us, including to post messages, upload information, communicate with us or take advantage of promotions, respond to surveys, or subscribe to newsletters or other mailing lists.
9. We may also collect information in the App where it is clear we are collecting such information.
10. We may use any or all of the above information to administer or operate the App, including proposing or administering forecasts on the App, identifying and fixing bugs or otherwise improving the performance of the App, making the App more useful to you or tailoring your experience with us to meet your special interests and expectations, fraud or legal protection, compliance or to improving or developing new features for the App or informing you about any of the same. We may also use this information in order to verify your age, location or other details for compliance or payment processing purposes. This may include, for instance, personal information, the last 4 digits of your social security number, or other identifying details about yourself. This information is used solely for the purpose of legal compliance, fraud detection and compliance with our policies, which may include age or geographic restrictions.
